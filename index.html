LINDSAY CORPORATION / 2018–2021

Crop science at commercial scale.

Thirty crop models. Satellite-informed phenology. Field trials.

MODELING & PRODUCT＋

As a FieldNET Advisor Solutions Specialist (Data Scientist), I developed and maintained 30 crop models, including corn, soybean, and cotton, for a commercial irrigation platform. I prototyped in Python, evaluated performance with R cross-validation, and combined crop simulations with satellite imagery to correct phenology estimates.

I led R&D trials measuring soil moisture, phenology, and yield, supervised interns, and translated findings for product and agronomy teams. I also developed an Azure CNN workflow for emergence and flowering detection.

03

CIBO TECHNOLOGIES / 2021–2024

Understand what the model is telling you.

SALUS sensitivity analysis and field-level agricultural data.

SYSTEMS & ANALYTICS＋

I evaluated SALUS responses to tillage, management, soil temperature and moisture, and planting and harvest dates. I integrated manure parameters, explored nitrogen-related outputs, and compared simulated and measured residue-related outputs to assess model behavior and identify limitations.

Alongside modeling, I built Python and Scala workflows on AWS to clean, quality-check, and tabulate agricultural data for Verra verification. I created observation-ingestion scripts and documented analyses in Confluence, with code contributed through GitHub pull requests.

SALUSSensitivity analysisPython · Scala · AWS

AGRICULTURAL CLIENT PROJECTS / 2017–2018

Better predictions. Clearer decisions.

Phenology, multi-location trials, and geospatial reporting.

AGRONOMIC ANALYTICS＋

On a Climate Corporation client project, I improved crop growth-stage prediction error from approximately ±7 days to ±3 days for a model deployed in a product. Across consulting projects for Climate Corporation, DuPont Pioneer, and Monsanto, I analyzed environmental drivers and genotype-by-environment effects using mixed models and variance components.

I processed gigabyte-scale field, trial, and climate data using Spark, Python, and R on AWS EC2, mapped results with QGIS and Tableau, and delivered weekly slide briefings to leadership.

Mixed modelsG×E analysisQGIS & Tableau
